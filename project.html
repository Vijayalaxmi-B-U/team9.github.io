About Our Project

This project presents a sustainable city design inspired by eco-friendly planning, smart infrastructure, and balanced land use. Our city map includes designated zones for residential living, agriculture, industries, education, healthcare, business parks, and green forest reserves. The design focuses on clean energy, smart water management, waste recycling, and climate-resilient development. With efficient road networks, public spaces, and community facilities, the city aims to provide a healthy, inclusive, and future-ready urban environment.

About the City

We chose the name VRITDHARA because it symbolizes the continuous flow and harmony of life, inspired by the circular design for our city. It reflects Completeness,Unity and the seamless connection between nature, people and infrastructure.

City Motto

  • "Craft in the essence of pi, forming eternal circles"
    • Inspired by infinite perfection of pi, every flow in harmonious circles. It symbolizes balance, and the city's vision of eternal growth.

    Location

    VRITDHARA is located near Mysore,Karnatak covering a total area of approximately 1500 sqKM

    City Planning and Zoning Overview

    Inner Zones

    • The central area consists of administrative and important public functions.
    • Surrounding inner zones include commercial areas and residential zones.
    • Residential areas are placed closer to commercial zones to reduce travel distance.
    • Essential services such as education, healthcare, and public facilities are within easy reach.

    Outer Zones

    • Industrial zones are placed towards the outer regions to reduce pollution impact.
    • Agricultural land is allocated in the outer belt to support food production.
    • Green zones act as buffers between industrial and residential areas.
    • Major transportation routes run along outer zones for smooth traffic flow.

    This zoning ensures minimum congestion, efficient land utilization, and environmental protection.
