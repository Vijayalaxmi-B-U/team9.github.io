What is the course about?

"The DAA (Design and Analysis of Algorithms) course has equipped us with systematic problem-solving skills to plan and optimize city designs. It enabled us to apply algorithmic thinking to develop efficient business ideas and urban solutions. Through this course, we have learned how algorithms guide decision-making for practical, real-world projects."

"In this course, we have studied various algorithms such as…"

"In this course, we explored key graph algorithms, including BFS, DFS, and shortest path techniques like Dijkstra's algorithm. Using these algorithms, we modeled and optimized city layouts, traffic flow, and infrastructure planning. This hands-on application of graph algorithms helped us design efficient and practical urban solutions, emphasizing the power of algorithmic thinking in real-world projects."
