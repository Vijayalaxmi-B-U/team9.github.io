Collaboration Overview

Rapid urbanization and industrial growth have increased pressure on water resources, leading to pollution, leakage, flooding risks, and inefficient distribution. Managing these challenges independently results in delayed responses and higher operational costs.

This collaboration integrates water quality monitoring with intelligent pipeline management to create a unified, data-driven water infrastructure for smart cities.

Smart Water Pollution Monitoring System (Sunil)

This system continuously monitors water quality and water levels in river–dam ecosystems. By dividing the river into zones and subzones, the system detects pollution, flood risks, and abnormal water behavior at an early stage.

Intelligent Water Flow & Leak Management System (Sushant)

This system focuses on optimizing urban water pipelines by minimizing leakage, reducing routing costs, and ensuring efficient water distribution using graph-based algorithms.

How the Collaboration Improves City Efficiency

Integrating pollution monitoring with pipeline management enables the city to make faster, more accurate, and cost-effective decisions. Instead of treating water quality and water distribution as separate problems, the system creates a shared data and decision layer.

  • Early detection of polluted or unsafe water prevents distribution through pipelines
  • Leak detection reduces water loss and conserves resources
  • Optimized routing minimizes energy usage and operational costs
  • Emergency zones are prioritized, reducing response time during floods or leaks
  • Unified monitoring improves coordination between water, dam, and city authorities

As a result, the city achieves improved water reliability, lower maintenance costs, enhanced public safety, and sustainable water management.

Algorithms Used in the Collaboration

  • Kruskal’s Algorithm – Minimum cost pipeline layout
  • Dijkstra’s Algorithm – Shortest water supply paths
  • Bellman–Ford Algorithm – Pollution spike and leak detection
  • Floyd–Warshall Algorithm – Optimize water flow between all junctions
  • Priority Queue – Emergency handling based on severity
  • Hash Table – Fast sensor-to-zone mapping
  • Queue – FIFO alert processing
  • Sorting Algorithms – Rank zones by pollution level and pressure drop
